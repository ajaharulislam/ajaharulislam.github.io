I am currently a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Institute of Particle Physics, Central China Normal University (CCNU), Wuhan, China.

I received my Ph.D. in Theoretical Nuclear Physics from Kent State University, USA, in 2024.

My research focuses on theoretical and computational high-energy nuclear physics, particularly heavy quarkonium in the quark-gluon plasma, quantum chromodynamics, heavy-ion collisions, and quantum computing applications in nuclear physics.

Research Interests

High-Energy Nuclear Physics Quark-Gluon Plasma Heavy Quarkonium Quantum Chromodynamics Quantum Computing Electron-Ion Collider Machine Learning Open Quantum Systems
