Research Overview

Our research focuses on theoretical, phenomenological, and computational high-energy nuclear physics, with particular emphasis on the production and dynamics of heavy quarkonia in quantum chromodynamics (QCD). We investigate heavy-quarkonium behavior in the quark-gluon plasma (QGP) created in relativistic heavy-ion collisions at RHIC and the LHC, as well as quarkonium production at the future Electron-Ion Collider (EIC).

We employ effective field theories, open quantum systems, and numerical simulations to understand these phenomena. We also explore machine learning and quantum computing as complementary tools for addressing challenging problems in nuclear physics.

01 pNRQCD and Open Quantum Systems

We study heavy-quarkonium dynamics in the quark-gluon plasma using potential nonrelativistic QCD (pNRQCD) and the open quantum systems framework. By incorporating interactions between heavy quark-antiquark pairs and the surrounding thermal medium, we investigate quarkonium suppression, dissociation, and transitions between quantum states.

pNRQCD Open Quantum Systems Lindblad Equation Heavy Quarkonium QGP

02 Real-Time Quantum Evolution

We investigate the real-time evolution of heavy-quarkonium states propagating through an evolving quark-gluon plasma. Using stochastic quantum trajectories and numerical solutions of quantum evolution equations, we study medium-induced transitions, quantum decoherence, and the time-dependent survival probabilities of quarkonium states in heavy-ion collisions.

Quantum Trajectories Real-Time Evolution Quantum Decoherence Quarkonium Suppression Numerical Simulations

03 Anisotropic Quark-Gluon Plasma

We investigate the properties of heavy quarkonia in anisotropic quark-gluon plasma, where the momentum distributions of medium constituents differ along different spatial directions. We study how momentum-space anisotropy modifies the heavy-quark potential, quarkonium binding energies, thermal widths, and suppression in relativistic heavy-ion collisions.

Anisotropic QGP Heavy-Quark Potential Binding Energies Thermal Widths Heavy-Ion Collisions

04 Heavy-Quarkonium Production at the EIC

We study exclusive and diffractive heavy-quarkonium production in electron-proton and electron-nucleus collisions, with applications to the future Electron-Ion Collider (EIC). By combining quarkonium wave functions with QCD descriptions of gluon distributions, we investigate the production of J/psi and psi(2S) states and their sensitivity to the gluonic structure of hadrons and nuclei.

Electron-Ion Collider Diffractive Production J/psi psi(2S) Gluon Distributions QCD

05 Machine Learning in Nuclear Physics

We explore machine learning methods for studying the properties of the quark-gluon plasma and heavy-quarkonium dynamics. By incorporating machine-learning-based predictions of medium properties into theoretical models and numerical simulations, we investigate their effects on heavy-quark interactions, quarkonium suppression, and transport phenomena.

Machine Learning QGP Properties Debye Screening Heavy Quarkonium Transport Phenomena

06 Quantum Algorithms and Computing for Nuclear Physics

We investigate quantum algorithms for solving computational problems in theoretical nuclear physics. Using variational quantum algorithms and quantum simulation techniques, we study heavy-quarkonium energy spectra and wave functions, explore quantum-state reconstruction from finite-shot measurements, and develop quantum computing approaches to observables relevant to heavy-quarkonium production.
